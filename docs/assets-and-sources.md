## Alt text

- **court:** White cubic house with deep recesses, a narrow path and a lawn beneath a blue sky.
- **atrium:** Concrete courtyard with a small tree, red window frames and glowing round pendant lights.
- **concrete:** Sunken concrete courtyard with a long stair, geometric patches of lawn and a blue sky.
- **facade:** Pale metal and concrete facade with an angular roof edge against a light gray sky.
- **interior:** Angular concrete interior with a timber bench and daylight entering through tall windows.
- **detail:** Warm timber walls and ceiling with narrow vertical openings above a concrete floor.
